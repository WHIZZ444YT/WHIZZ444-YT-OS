| Podcasts | Label nine full episodes and two clips. Store program, host, guest, topic, duration and chapters where confirmed. Keep unverified names and timestamps visible as editorial gaps. |
| Mobile and keyboard | Allow a wrapping subnavigation or accessible quick selector at 360–390 CSS px. Retain active tab, search and filters on Back. Avoid focus traps and scroll jumps. Support Tab/Shift+Tab, Enter, Escape for dismissible panels and legible focus in both themes. |
| Video viewing | Show the lesson explanation and example first. Click opens a direct video or an optional embed with fallback. Reserve embed space and lazy-load players on intent. Render editorial blocks and basic filters quickly without loading ten embedded players. |

Academy records. It derives mapped and open counts from the Academy and labels
video evidence independently of candidate or related-source status. The Learn
and Playbooks filters preserve their selections in the URL, while direct links
keep the written content available without JavaScript. Mentor profiles link to
matching lessons. The mentor Popular filter appears only when at least one
profile has comparable publisher-verified snapshots.

Direct watch links are available without loading a player. Live chapter,
playback, and embed checks remain open where the registry lacks observations.
